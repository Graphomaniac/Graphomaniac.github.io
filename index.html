About me

I am a graduate student and a Vanier Scholar at University of Toronto.

My main areas of interest are derived differential geometry and toric geometry, especially as it connects to complex geometry. My advisors are Prof. Nick Rozenblyum of UofT and Prof. Dmitri Pavlov of TTU.
